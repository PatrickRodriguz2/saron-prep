SARON Prep for iOS

Support

A study aid for the ATPL(A) SARON written examination — air law and procedures, aeroplane systems and operation, and general navigation.

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections to the question bank are especially welcome — please include the question and the reference you're working from (TC AIM section, CAR number, or TP 690E).

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 17.0 or later. The app works entirely offline; no internet connection is needed at any point.

How the app works

Practise by topic with instant feedback, or sit a full 80-question mock exam under the real 3½-hour time limit with the pass mark at 70%. Every answer is tracked: missed questions come back on a spaced schedule in Smart Review, and the Home tab estimates your readiness by section and topic. The Load & Trim Trainer generates a fresh load and trim sheet with questions computed from it — zero fuel weight, per cent MAC and the weight-shift problem — and the Calculation Drills generate ISA, density altitude, fuel, crosswind, convergency, time zone and descent problems with worked solutions.

Progress and resetting

All progress lives on your device only. To start over, use "Reset all progress" at the bottom of the Library tab. Deleting the app also deletes all progress — there is no cloud copy.

A question looks wrong?

Every question carries an explanation and a source reference. If you believe an answer conflicts with the current TC AIM, CARs or CAP, email the question text and your reference — verified corrections ship in the next update. Always treat the current official publications as authoritative.
